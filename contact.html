Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting our quiet estuary sanctuary.

What are the check-in and check-out timings? expand_more

Standard check-in begins at 11:00 AM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late luggage drop can be coordinated via WhatsApp prior to arrival.

Are the rooms equipped with an air conditioner? expand_more

Yes, our guest rooms are equipped with efficient air conditioners to keep you cool and comfortable throughout your coastal stay.

How far is Kumta Beach? expand_more

Kumta Beach is just 150m from our front gate, making it easy to enjoy a quick walk to the shore.

What is the cancellation policy? expand_more

For direct bookings via WhatsApp or email, free cancellation is provided up to 5 days prior to arrival. Airbnb bookings follow our standard Flexible listing policy.

Is parking available at the property? expand_more

Yes, complimentary on-site secure parking is available for cars and two-wheelers.
