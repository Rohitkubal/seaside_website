About Seaside

Tucked quietly along the pristine coastline of Kumta in Uttara Kannada, Seaside Guest House is an authentic sanctuary for wanderers seeking solace. Built inspired by coastal vernacular architecture, our home blends warm aged teak accents with cool stone floors and open-air corridors capturing the invigorating Arabian sea breeze.

Here, time gracefully slows to the rhythm of gentle tides. Stroll through our coconut groves down to where the quiet river confluence meets the sea, enjoy fresh local coastal hospitality, and recharge in spaces thoughtfully crafted to feel like your private home by the shore.
